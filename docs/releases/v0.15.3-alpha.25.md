# v0.15.3-alpha.25 — marker click wheel and small-raid Apply

The optional marker wheel now uses one key plus an explicit icon click. Releasing without an icon click always requests the next marker at the cursor, regardless of mouse travel or hold time. Hold and complete a click on a visible icon, then click terrain to choose a specific marker. Its matching held-key release is consumed. An interrupted icon press cancels. The clickable wheel appears immediately and can flash briefly on taps. No unsupported protected timer or invisible selection controls are used.

Raid Groups automatically matches unique full-name late joins, coalesces redundant tray copies and retains unused unmatched tray placeholders without blocking complete present-player targets. One/two-player organized raids and large absent templates are supported. Grouped ambiguous identities have a visible Match button; conflicting targets, unassigned current players and fresh roster/combat/authority/capacity/ACK guards remain.

Marker snapshots/history/custom order/Clear and alpha.24 realm aliases are retained. Schema 19, catalog 6 and readiness protocol 1 remain. No native placement, click-through, taint or subgroup server acceptance is claimed. Native owner testing remains open. CurseForge, tags and releases remain held.
