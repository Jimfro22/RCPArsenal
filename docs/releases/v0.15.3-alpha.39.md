# 0.15.3-alpha.39 Death Log timeline

The selected-health bar, percentage and raw current/max now sit below the timeline. The separate recorded-HP plot and healing-debt overlay are removed. Hovering or scrolling a damage cluster selects the inspected hit's actual recorded HP while its largest-known-hit artwork stays fixed. Only the explicitly fictional Preview can show a validated effective-healing segment; live absorbed damage never becomes healing.

Nearby damage from different sources and abilities groups within a fixed half-second window anchored at the first hit. Totals and counts retain every member; the actual killing blow is identified independently of the representative icon. Partial totals remain lower bounds. The default five-second view extends to a whole second, up to eight, for an older nonfatal hit worth at least20% of recorded maximum HP or50% of the largest known nonfatal hit within eight seconds. Older retained events remain in the full report and are disclosed in info hover.

Defensive casts now have explicitly approximate death-aligned markers. Their visible `~` and hover explain alignment to the death notification; repeated receipts stay distinct, while offscreen or unaligned uses remain accessible in recorded-use details. Missing or stale anchors never become zero-time casts. No effect duration or availability is inferred from use.

Cooldown icons use drawn check/cross badges for proven frozen states and question marks for unresolved states. Check ages remain in hover. Available Healthstones require known remaining uses. The alpha.38 entry card and the full-report/chat workflow remain intact.

This is a review candidate. Native rendering, input, tooltip behavior and timing still need in-game acceptance. CurseForge, tags and releases remain held.
