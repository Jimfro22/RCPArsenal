# 0.15.3-alpha.38 Death Log entry card

The Death Log card is centered above Blizzard's death popup. A compact strip shows only confidently available frozen checks, ordered as major defensives, minor defensives, Healthstone and healing potion. Clicking the card or an icon opens the captured Death Log. Icon tooltips show the captured name, availability evidence and its age before the local death notice; the icons do not cast abilities or use items.

The strip says **Available when checked**, because the recorded checks may predate death. Unknown, pending and unavailable checks are omitted. Healthstone requires a positively recorded remaining-use count. Empty states distinguish **No availability confirmed** from **Availability was not recorded**. Late native hit data does not replace the strip's frozen evidence. The full Death Log and its Preview are unchanged.

`/rcp recapentrypreview` shows a clearly labelled sample card outside combat without requiring another death. Its icons open the existing sample Death Log. The preview does not consume a real death's attention state.

The card retains the existing guarded death/page/native-popup lifecycle and subtle attention animation. It creates no new scanner, ticker or cooldown queries. This is a test candidate; native placement, tooltips and protected-state behavior still require in-game acceptance. CurseForge, tags and releases remain held.
