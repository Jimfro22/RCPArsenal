# Raid Groups (alpha.24)

Open Raid Groups beside Quick Bar in the sidebar, or use `/rcp groups`. `/rcp raid` keeps its existing Addons destination. All eight groups appear in four columns and two rows, with five slots each. These slots plan subgroup membership; Blizzard raid frames may order people differently within a subgroup.

Drag a card to a slot, or select it and click its destination. Dropping on an occupied slot swaps the two cards locally. The unassigned tray retains current players without a target; every current player must have a group before Apply. Cards show offline, absent and unresolved status, duplicate-name realms and full-identity tooltips. Right-click an absent/unresolved card to match an exact current raider or remove an absent card. No current raider can be removed from the raid through this pane.

From raid explicitly replaces the draft with current groups. Update raid refreshes and matches exact full-name/realm identities while retaining staged slots. It never guesses a short name. Unlisted current players remain visible, including in the tray when their current group has no free draft slot.

Give a draft a boss/layout name, then Save. A persistent caption and highlighted Save indicate unsaved content. Moving cards back to the saved positions clears it; live groups, roles, offline status, indices and Apply acknowledgements do not count as saved edits. Only staged content is persisted; newly observed, unadopted tray cards do not silently enter Save. Loading may adopt additional current raiders into the draft; if this differs from the stored layout, it remains unsaved. From raid starts a new unsaved draft. Editing a saved name uses Save as and retains the original; an existing different name is rejected, including case variants. Load that layout to deliberately update it. Failed saves retain the prior baseline. Load explicitly replaces local unsaved edits.

Named layouts survive profile import/reset under `RaidArsenalDB.raidGroupLayouts`, outside portable profiles. Storage holds up to 50 layouts with 80 identities each, using plain identities rather than session GUIDs or raid indices. The active unsaved draft is session-local. There is no automatic boss selection or encounter application.

Import WowUtils accepts plain exported text: `EncounterID:123;Difficulty:Heroic;Name:Boss` headers and comma-positioned `invitelist:` rows with an optional terminal semicolon. Empty positions remain holes. Read sections, explicitly select one and Import & save. The proposed unique local name is shown; collisions get a bounded suffix such as `(2)` and never replace another layout. Success commits the draft and saved entry together, updates the actual name field and makes it available in Load. Malformed, duplicate, oversized, invalid-storage or full-store failures preserve the prior draft, saved entries and baseline, with the import dialog still available. Changed paste invalidates the selection. Absent names and unresolved short names round-trip without inventing a realm. There is no URL fetch, bearer key, invite, kick, chat broadcast or Northern Sky action.

Absent placeholders do not need to be deleted to Apply. Fresh review checks every current member, including offline and already-assigned people. A plain short-name card with no possible current match stays in its group slot or tray and is disclosed as skipped; it creates no live target. One or more possible matches require explicit full-name matching, even when that current player already has another full-name card. Incomplete names, uncertain text comparisons or unavailable roster data cannot prove absence. Qualified absent names remain placeholders too. Cards distinguish Absent from Needs matching, with a right-click explanation. Move a placeholder to the local tray if a current raider needs its group slot; no hidden bench or parking action occurs.

Imported realm aliases (alpha.24) use the same bounded comparison key in capture, import, saved-layout loading, reconciliation and Apply review. Only realm whitespace, ASCII apostrophes and internal hyphens are folded; character names/accents and realm Unicode are preserved. This is an Arsenal import-alias policy, not a verified Blizzard punctuation-removal algorithm. Native APIs do not document that algorithm in the reviewed UI source. Matching still requires one unique current roster identity and fresh GUID. Different realms and ambiguous short names remain distinct; colliding live alias keys and duplicate group targets block rather than choose arbitrarily. Tooltips disclose a display spelling matched to another current full name, and the review counts aliases.

Older layouts may contain an imported display spelling in a group plus its native-spelling duplicate in the tray. Loading coalesces only that same unique current GUID's redundant tray alias. Two group targets or truly absent cards are never silently merged. The saved entry remains unchanged until explicit Save, with a visible Save-needed note that clears after saving.

Present players without an explicit target still block Apply, including when all five/eight groups look full of template cards. The footer names the player and current group and explains how to place their Unassigned card. Dropping it on an absent card swaps the absent card into the tray, retaining it; no deletion or arbitrary destination is required. Only actual current GUID targets count toward live group capacity.

Sort draft opens a compact menu above the unassigned tray. Role sort fills free slots in Tank → Melee → Ranged → Healer order. Odd/even and adjacent splits balance known roles across two sides, using the first 2, 4, 6 or 8 groups that fit; adjacent means two contiguous blocks such as G1+2 / G3+4. These are local role-based layouts, without the Quick Bar optimizer's extra class/spec/movement scoring. Existing absent/unresolved group cards stay in their exact slots, and explicit tray entries stay in the tray. All identities are retained and each group has at most five slots. Unknown classifications or insufficient preserved-slot capacity refuse the whole local operation with an explanation. No inspection starts. Current assigned Tank/Healer roles are sufficient; Damage uses already validated same-identity reports or an unambiguous class fallback. Hunter, Shaman, Druid and Demon Hunter damage remain unknown without a usable spec. Cached spec use is disclosed because it can be old. Sorting invalidates the prior preview; explicit Review/Apply remains the route to live changes.

Cards show a small tank/healer icon only for a readable current assigned role. Unknown, absent and unresolved cards have no invented role icon. Role updates are observed only while the planner is visible/applying. Optional unreadable/secret role values do not invalidate otherwise readable identities. Fixed icons use Blizzard's [micro role atlas names](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/b9be9379719047977fca4dba1bb1d9988e8840b3/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_SharedXMLBase/TextureUtil.lua). Raid composition statistics remain deferred.

Review changes captures a fresh roster and displays the complete current-to-target mapping. Apply to raid requires that exact eligible preview, a player-organized raid, leader/assistant authority, known out-of-combat members, no active encounter and no competing Quick Bar sort. Ordinary draft editing does not require authority. Every current GUID needs exactly one explicit group target; present unassigned members, duplicate targets and unresolved possible current identities still block Apply. A matching player joining after review invalidates the preview before submission; review and explicitly match again. No automatic realm resolution occurs.

The footer beside Apply keeps the exact blocker visible ahead of Save or selection messages. Ready previews disclose skipped absent people. If the raid already matches the draft, Apply is disabled because there are no live group changes to send. Save or unrelated layout deletion does not erase a failed-review reason; changed draft/roster/eligibility invalidates it and requires a fresh review. Closing and reopening prepares again.

Apply submits one subgroup move or swap at a time, using a fresh GUID-to-index lookup. Only the exact observed roster acknowledgement permits the next operation. Combat, lost authority, changed membership, unexpected group changes or a finite acknowledgement timeout stop the job. It never retries or resumes automatically. Cancel and closing the pane stop future submissions; a request already sent may still settle. Confirmed progress and stopped/partial results remain visible. There is no planner live Undo in this release.

An unresolved submitted request fences both new Apply and Quick Bar sorting. Review raid refreshes and displays the observed full-identity groups. Acknowledge groups recaptures and requires that exact fingerprint plus current eligibility, preserves the draft and sends no subgroup call. It clears the fence only after explicit acknowledgement; a prior native request may still settle. Review changes and a new Apply are required afterward. Any unexpected late result stops a subsequent job. Closing an unresolved planner leaves no background polling; reopening or explicit refresh can observe settlement.

Quick Bar sorting now exposes Cancel while waiting for inspection as well as while sorting. Cancelling clears its pending mode, so later inspection/report callbacks cannot start it. Both controllers reject competing group work. Profile changes stop pending/active work. General, unrelated pages and hidden planner callbacks do not activate roster observation; the planner observes through native events only while visible or applying.

## Windows acceptance

Update the existing checkout with Fetch origin → Pull origin, then fully restart WoW. No saved-variable reset, reinstall or junction change is needed. Offline Lua mocks verify addon decisions; the following native checks remain required:

1. Open `/rcp groups`; verify eight readable groups at your UI scale, duplicate realms, class colors, offline cards, scrolling and full-name tooltips. Drag to empty/occupied slots, drop outside, and use click-select destinations. Check that no live group changes before Apply.
2. Save/load a named boss layout; paste a note with multiple sections and empty positions. Confirm explicit section choice, absent cards and exact matching after a player joins. Update raid must preserve staged slots. Check long paste/list scrolling, Escape, focus cleanup and closing/reopening Arsenal.
3. As a raid member, draft normally but confirm Apply is unavailable. As leader/assistant out of combat, review a small known change and Apply once. Check the native groups and progress after each acknowledgement, including a swap of full groups. No frame-order guarantee is intended.
4. Cancel during Apply; test combat, lost authority, roster churn and another person's group edit. Future requests must stop, the draft must remain and no job may auto-resume. If an acknowledgement does not arrive, review observed groups and explicitly acknowledge them before a fresh review. The warning must disclose that an older request may still settle.
5. Start Quick Bar sorting while roles are unknown, cancel its inspection wait and let inspection finish. It must stay cancelled. During planner Apply or unresolved results, old Sort/Split/Undo must not start. During old pending/active sorting, planner Apply must remain unavailable and become reviewable after cancellation.
6. Confirm General stays quiet, last-page reopening and the placement shelf work, `/rcp raid` still opens Addons, and profile import/reset retains named layouts. Recheck alpha.18 marker center/sector/cancel behavior and existing glow, health and audio settings. Watch for native Lua errors, protected-action blocks and taint.

Rename for Save as, return to the original name, test an existing-name rejection, and reload to discard edits. Live role/offline/group changes should not alone mark saved content dirty. Import the same boss twice; both unique layouts must appear in Load, including absent/unresolved cards after reload. Exercise all three Sort draft commands and an unknown-role refusal; verify no real groups move until explicit Apply. Check tank/healer icons, unknown/empty row reuse and the popover's covered-card click/drop handling at your UI scale.
