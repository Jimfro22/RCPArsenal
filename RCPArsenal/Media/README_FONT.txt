RCP Arsenal does not distribute third-party font files.

The addon automatically uses Expressway when another installed addon registers
it through LibSharedMedia. You may also place your own licensed copies at:

  Interface\AddOns\RCPArsenal\Media\Expressway.TTF
  Interface\AddOns\RCPArsenal\Media\Expressway Bold.ttf

Then run /reload. Without those files, RCP Arsenal uses Blizzard Arial Narrow.
